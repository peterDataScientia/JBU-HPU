# S5 — Evidence appraisal

The evidence-appraisal table presents six criterion-level assessments for each of the 13 included studies (78 assessments). Ratings are **Fulfilled**, **Partially fulfilled**, or **Not established**. The study-level table and criterion-level data provide the final documentary assessments and supporting evidence, with source locations for traceability.

The assessments characterize information documented in the included publications. **Not established** means the source report did not establish the criterion; it is not a finding that the underlying procedure was necessarily absent. No claim of a formal consensus meeting or coauthor approval is made.
