# JBU–HPU urease potency systematic review: Supplementary materials

These files accompany the systematic review *Cross-Species Transferability of Urease Inhibitor Potency: A Systematic Review of Jack Bean and Helicobacter pylori Urease Assays*.

- **S1** — Literature search strategies.
- **S2** — PRISMA study-selection accounting and full-text exclusion ledger (103 records).
- **S3** — Paired inhibitory concentrations (46 observations from 13 studies), with original endpoint designations and harmonized µM units.
- **S4** — Study characteristics and assay conditions.
- **S5** — Study-level and criterion-level evidence appraisal (78 assessments), including source locations.
- **S6** — Statistical reanalysis, study-level results, and leave-one-study-out sensitivity analyses.

The source-reported precision is retained; unspecified error-bar types are not relabeled as SD or SEM. The supplementary data and scripts document the quantitative synthesis and study-selection records.

For the S4 assay-stage description, inhibitor preincubation, urea hydrolysis, and colour development are distinct steps. Odake (1994) used different HPU and JBU analytical readouts. Calculated final urea concentrations of 64.7 mM (Mohammed 2022) and 16.7 mM (Li 2025) refer to the hydrolysis mixture *before* the addition of colour-development reagents. NR means not reported in the examined assay protocol and must not be interpreted as zero.

## Repository formats

All six supplementary sections (S1–S6) are published as directly readable Markdown, CSV, JSON or Python files in [`supplementary/`](supplementary/). The formatted Word presentation of S5 is not included as a binary DOCX in this repository; its appraisal entries are available in the detailed S5 CSV files and the readable S5 Markdown summary.
